Sahaj · Resident app · React Native

Everything a resident does, in twenty-six screens.

One rule holds the app together: any number it shows, it can explain. Core tasks sit within three taps of home, hit targets never fall below 44 points, and dues, notices and passes stay readable with no network.

05 · Who you are

One person, many units

The same login can be an owner of several flats, a tenant in someone else's, or both at once. The app resolves this with a unit context: one flat is active at a time, and that flat's relationship decides what the screen offers.
