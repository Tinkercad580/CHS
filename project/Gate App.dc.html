Sahaj · Gate app · React Native, offline-first

Built for a guard, in the dark, with no signal.

A separate app on a bound device. Larger type, larger targets, dark ground for night shifts, and a full 24 hours of autonomy — every entry queues locally and syncs on reconnect. It never calls a finance endpoint, so no permission mistake can expose money or a resident's real phone number.
